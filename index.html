FAQ

Questions clients usually ask

How quickly can you start?

Usually within a week of our first call. Advisory work can often start in a few days.

Is there a minimum engagement?

No long lock-ins. Part-time plans run month to month, and project work is scoped up front.

How is pricing done?

Hourly, block-of-hours, monthly retainer or fixed project price, whichever suits you. We quote after the free architecture call, once we understand the scope.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work remotely with teams in India, the Middle East, Europe and the US, and adjust hours for overlap.

How do you handle access and security?

NDA first, least-privilege access through your SSO or IAM, MFA everywhere, and all changes made through code review.

What if we only need a one-off review?

That's common. A cloud cost, security or reliability review with a written report is a great place to start.
